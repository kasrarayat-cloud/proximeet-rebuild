**Direction:** A Place Opens  
**Scope:** Welcome only. Visual design and the illustrated motion study approved by the founder on 2026-09-24: “Perfect i approve go to next chapter.” This approves the design reference and two-dimensional pose sequence, not a production 3D rig, authentication, or later screens.  
**Editable design:** https://www.figma.com/design/9ZkCIlu9AqAD1f92UDuMAF

## The experience

A tactile ivory invitation opens into a connected miniature café, music room and planted terrace. The steel clasp and cyan woven loop introduce the metal-and-fabric language intended for later intent bands and approach signals.

The scene communicates an invitation to social places. It is illustrative: it does not represent a real venue, live attendance or nearby people.

## Three review poses

1. **Closed:** ivory invitation, blind-debossed architecture, steel clasp, cyan woven loop.
2. **Opening:** lifted paper cover reveals the café while the upper rooms remain partly concealed.
3. **Settled:** connected café, music room and planted terrace form one readable composition.

The settled pose also provides the reduced-motion direction.

## Motion status and production boundary

The review animation is a two-dimensional timing study using generated key-pose artwork. The downloadable MP4 is rendered locally from the actual Figma key-pose screens; an editable timeline is also available in the Figma file. Figma produced a native video export, but its download endpoint was unavailable in this session. The delivered clip demonstrates sequence, scale, placement and pacing. It is not a continuous, physically simulated fold, an editable 3D model, or a verified iOS implementation.

The four-second review clip holds the closed pose for 0.4 seconds, transitions through the opening pose to the settled scene over the following one second, then holds the final screen. Text and controls remain visible throughout. These are design timings, not measured device performance.

The image-generation tool produced the artwork. Figma supplies editable layout, text, controls and animation composition. The first bounded implementation may reproduce the approved two-dimensional pose sequence using these exact assets. A continuous physical fold requires a separate, consistent rigged asset with authored hinges and materials. The current images alone cannot prove mechanical continuity.

Real-device performance, accessibility behavior, sign-in and navigation remain implementation work. Motion must be interruptible; reduced motion should show the settled scene without an unfolding sequence. Final accessibility verification must include large text, readable contrast, assistive labels, and target sizes.
