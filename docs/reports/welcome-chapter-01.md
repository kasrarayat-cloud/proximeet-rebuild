## Layout

Rows are placed by a small custom `Layout` on the approved gaps. The hero takes the remaining
height, up to a full-width square. On a 440 × 956 screen whose top safe inset is at most 62 pt,
the geometry works out to the spec exactly: brand 64–96, hero 0,128 440 × 440, headline 570–688,
supporting line 704–729, button 772–832, "More options" 844–888, "18+ only" 913–929. These
figures are **derived from the layout code**, not measured on a device.

On other screens (derived, unverified), the hero is about 358 pt on a 402 × 874 iPhone and about
151 pt on a 375 × 667 iPhone SE. Past a 140 pt minimum the page scrolls instead of overlapping.

Figma's 59 pt headline line height is tighter than Manrope's natural 76.5 pt line. Each
headline line therefore gets a 59 pt box with its glyphs centred, which reproduces Figma's
baselines. Text that has to wrap at larger Dynamic Type sizes uses the natural line height, so
it never overlaps.

## Motion (2D pose study; not 3D, not a continuous fold)

- **Sequence.** Closed holds 0–0.4 s, blends linearly to opening by 0.85 s, then to settled by 1.4 s, and holds.
- **Once only.** It runs once per Welcome instance and never loops.
- **Why linear.** The approved study measures linear at 30 fps: a constant per-frame change over 0.40–0.87 s and 0.87–1.43 s.
- **Blending.** Every pose is drawn on the page colour, so each fade blends whole composed frames the way the study does.
- **Declarative.** It uses scoped, opacity-only animations with delays, with no timers or tasks.
- **Leaving the screen** completes it at once, so nothing replays or keeps animating.
- **Reduce Motion** shows the settled pose from the first frame.
- **Accessibility.** The hero is hidden from VoiceOver and takes no touches. Text and buttons are present and usable throughout.

## Buttons (actual behaviour)

- **Continue with Apple.** Shows the alert "Apple sign-in isn’t connected" with "This prototype doesn’t connect to Apple yet. Nothing was sent or saved." and OK. No authentication API is called, and no state or session changes.
- **More options.** Shows the alert "More options aren’t available" with "This prototype doesn’t include other sign-in methods yet." and OK. No state changes. No options flow exists in this repository.
- **18+ only.** Informational text, not a control.

## Fidelity deviations and assumptions

1. The **Apple mark** is the SF Symbol `apple.logo`, sized to the reference ink (14 × 16 in a 22 × 26 box). The handoff has no vector for it.
2. The button is the approved **custom control**, not `SignInWithAppleButton`: tapping the system control starts a real authorization request. If Apple sign-in is ever integrated, review this against Apple's Sign in with Apple guidelines.
3. **Brand row position.** It is at `max(top safe inset + 2, 64)`. This assumes the iPhone 17 Pro Max's top inset is at most 62 pt; if it is larger, the brand row sits 2 pt below it.
4. On screens **shorter than the reference**, the hero shrinks (it is centred and never cropped), while text and gaps keep their approved sizes.
5. At the **largest text sizes**, the wordmark shrinks to fit on one line, and headline lines that wrap use Manrope's natural line height.
6. Once the sequence ends, the **three pose layers stay composed** until Welcome disappears. Memory use is unmeasured.
7. `CTFontManagerRegisterGraphicsFont` may be marked deprecated from iOS 18. With the 17.0 deployment target it should not warn, but it would need replacing if the target rises.
8. A custom "Continue with Apple" button that does not sign in is fine for this local prototype. It could draw questions in Beta App Review if the build ever reaches external TestFlight testers.

The approved Welcome has no demo entry, and both of its controls are honest notices. So
Birthday and the later scenes are unreachable in the app except through DEBUG fixtures
(`-ProximeetFixture birthday`, …).

**Direction (2026-09-25), documented but not implemented:**

- More options → "Continue the demo (no account)" → the **approved Birthday** (chapter 02).
- Back returns to a settled Welcome, which does not replay.
- The Birthday draft stays in memory across that back-and-forward navigation.
- It never creates authentication or verification state.

It is built together with the approved Birthday, never by exposing the legacy `BirthdayView`
that the fixtures still reach. That work branches from the lead's Welcome commit, **never from
`7af76fa`**, which does not contain Welcome.
