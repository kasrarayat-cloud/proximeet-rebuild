## Observed gaps

- B1 uses flat strips rather than complete curved woven loops. A first-launch Apple Intelligence notification obscures part of the first capture.
- B1/B2 headings are too large, uppercase and condensed. The reference hierarchy is centered, heavy, normal-width and sentence case.
- B2's large portrait cards repeat descriptions; the lower row is partly outside the initial viewport. Use compact landscape plaques, short native labels and one selected explanation.
- Material depth is weak: repeated procedural weave, oversized clasps and flat metal. Reuse a coherent woven-loop asset, fine brushed plate and consistent native hardware.
- Ready needs a compact assembled object, readable paper summary and secondary reset action. The selected band and signal must remain consistent with B1/B2.
- Welcome, Birthday, Pass and Guest List have no first-build native image evidence. The next run must capture them before any visual approval.
